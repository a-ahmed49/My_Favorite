Fanpage : Minecraft

Welcome to my fanpage.

Here you can find some information about my favorite game : Minecraft

I really like this game, because there are no limits to what you can do.

You can build castles, farms and even redstone machines!

You can also explore caves and survive the night with friends!

Minecraft was created by Markus "Notch" Persson and developed by Mojang.

The full version was released in 2011, and Microsoft bought Mojang in 2014.

It is one of the best-selling video games of all time.

Facts summarized in my own words. For more, see Minecraft on Wikipedia.
